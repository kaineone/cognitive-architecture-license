# Changes in the CAL 0.5 working draft

Version 0.5 keeps every protection of version 0.4 and changes the legal machinery around them, so that the license is more likely to be enforceable as a copyright license. None of these changes has yet been reviewed by counsel.

## Grant and conditions

- Explicit grant (2.1). The Licensor and every Contributor grant the rights in so many words, for the term of the copyright.
- Acceptance (2.2). Exercising a right accepts the license; without acceptance there is no permission beyond what the law allows anyway.
- Conditions of the grant (2.3). The obligations in Articles 3 to 6 are conditions of the copyright grant, so using the Software in breach of them falls outside the license and can be pursued as infringement, which is the strongest remedy a license can carry.

## Sharing, patents, and marks

- Distribution and network use (2.4). Recipients of a Derivative Work get its Corresponding Source, and people who interact with a modified version over a network are offered its source, following the model of the GNU Affero General Public License.
- Defensive patent termination (2.5). A party that sues claiming the Software infringes a patent loses its patent licenses under CAL, following the model of the Apache License 2.0.
- No trademark rights (2.6).

## Definitions

- New terms: You (including affiliates), Contributor, Contribution, Distribute, Corresponding Source, Mutual-Aid Organization (used in Article 5.1 but undefined in 0.4), and Adoption Notice.

## Anchors in existing law

- Prohibited uses (3.5) are also read by reference to Article 5 of the EU Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) where it applies.
- Disclosure (6.2) adds to legal disclosure duties such as Article 50 of that Regulation.

## Termination, warranty, and administration

- Notice and cure (8.4). Written notice starts the thirty-day cure period.
- Downstream recipients (8.5) keep their licenses when an upstream licensee's license ends.
- Warranty and liability (9.1, 9.2) are disclaimed conspicuously and to the maximum extent the law allows, without excluding liability the law does not let a license exclude.
- Adoption Notice (10.3, 10.4). Each Licensor names itself, its Steward, the governing law, and an address for notices, so the governing law is never undefined.
- Entire license and notices (10.5, 10.6).
